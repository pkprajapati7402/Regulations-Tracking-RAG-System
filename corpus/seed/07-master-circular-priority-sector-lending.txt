Master Directions - Priority Sector Lending (PSL) - Targets and Classification

01 April 2024

To
The Chairman / Managing Director / Chief Executive Officer of All Commercial Banks (including Small Finance Banks, Local Area Banks, Regional Rural Banks, Urban Co-operative Banks and Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks)

Madam/Dear Sir,

These Directions consolidate the guidelines on Priority Sector Lending and shall be applicable from 01 April 2024.

1. Categories under priority sector
1.1 The categories under priority sector are: Agriculture, Micro Small and Medium Enterprises, Export Credit, Education, Housing, Social Infrastructure, Renewable Energy and Others.

2. Targets and sub-targets

[TABLE]
Category | Domestic commercial banks and foreign banks with 20 branches and above | Regional Rural Banks | Small Finance Banks | Urban Co-operative Banks
Total Priority Sector | 40 per cent of Adjusted Net Bank Credit | 75 per cent of ANBC | 75 per cent of ANBC | 60 per cent of ANBC
Agriculture | 18 per cent of ANBC | 18 per cent of ANBC | 18 per cent of ANBC | Not applicable
Small and Marginal Farmers | 10 per cent of ANBC | 10 per cent of ANBC | 10 per cent of ANBC | Not applicable
Micro Enterprises | 7.5 per cent of ANBC | 7.5 per cent of ANBC | 7.5 per cent of ANBC | 7.5 per cent of ANBC
Weaker Sections | 12 per cent of ANBC | 15 per cent of ANBC | 12 per cent of ANBC | 12 per cent of ANBC
[/TABLE]

3. Housing loans eligible for priority sector classification
3.1 Loans to individuals up to rupees thirty-five lakh in metropolitan centres with a population of ten lakh and above, and loans up to rupees twenty-five lakh in other centres, for purchase or construction of a dwelling unit per family, are eligible for classification under priority sector, provided the overall cost of the dwelling unit does not exceed rupees forty-five lakh and rupees thirty lakh respectively.
3.2 Loans for repairs to damaged dwelling units of families up to rupees ten lakh in metropolitan centres and up to rupees six lakh in other centres are eligible.

4. Education loans
4.1 Loans to individuals for educational purposes, including vocational courses, not exceeding rupees twenty-five lakh, are eligible for classification as priority sector, irrespective of the sanctioned amount limits applicable earlier.

5. Renewable energy
5.1 Bank loans up to a limit of rupees thirty-five crore to borrowers for purposes such as solar based power generators, biomass based power generators, wind mills, micro-hydel plants and non-conventional energy based public utilities are eligible.
5.2 For individual households, the loan limit is rupees ten lakh per borrower.

6. Monitoring and non-achievement
6.1 Priority sector lending achievement shall be monitored on a quarterly as well as an annual basis, computed as the average of credit outstanding at the close of business on the last date of each quarter.
6.2 Banks having a shortfall in lending to the priority sector target shall be allocated amounts for contribution to the Rural Infrastructure Development Fund established with NABARD and other funds with NABARD, NHB, SIDBI and MUDRA Ltd, as decided by the Reserve Bank from time to time.
